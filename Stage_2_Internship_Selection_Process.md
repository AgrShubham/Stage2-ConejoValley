# Internship Assignment – Cloning & Creative Redesign

## 🎯 Objective

You will **clone a homepage**, then **redesign it with a new theme, copy, and images**, while preserving the original layout.

This mirrors real work we do for clients.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Next.js + Tailwind CSS (required)**
- You may use any component libraries
- AI tools (ChatGPT, Cursor, Bolt, etc.) are allowed and encouraged, but use them intelligently—refine manually; no blind copy-paste.

⚠️ **Important:**

AI is allowed, but blind copy-paste is not. We want people who **use AI intelligently**, then refine manually.

---

## 🌐 Website to Clone

You will clone the **homepage** of this template: [https://www.conejovalleycounseling.com/home](https://www.conejovalleycounseling.com/home)

---

## 🧩 Assignment Breakdown

### **Part 1: Clone the Homepage (UI Accuracy Test)**

**Goal:** Test your frontend and UI replication skills.

You must:

- Recreate the **layout, spacing, structure, and responsiveness**
- Match section order, grid systems, and hierarchy
- Ensure mobile responsiveness

**Checklist for Completion:**

- [ ] My clone looks exactly like the original [https://www.conejovalleycounseling.com/home](https://www.conejovalleycounseling.com/home) homepage in terms of layout and structure.
- [ ] It is fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile views.
- [ ] It follows the same fonts, typography, and styling as the original.
- [ ] I have used reusable themes/colors (e.g., via Tailwind CSS classes or CSS variables) for easy maintainability.
- [ ] I have ensured consistent spacing, padding, and margins throughout.

---

### **Part 2: Redesign Using an imaginary therapist’s Profile**

Once the homepage is cloned, you will redesign it using [Dr. Maya Reynolds’ profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing) as the *single source of truth* for content.

### 🔍 Dr. Maya Reynolds (Imaginary Therapist) - [Profile Link](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)

You will be evaluated on **how accurately and thoughtfully you extract information from the profile**.

### 🎨 Theme & Color Palette

- Replace the existing color palette with a **new cohesive theme**
- Choose a primary, secondary, and accent color

We are evaluating your **design eye**, not just technical ability. The new color palette should look aesthetic and match the overall design vibes.

**Checklist for Theme & Colors:**

- [ ] I have replaced the existing theme
- [ ] I have selected a new color palette different from the current palette (primary, secondary, accent) that is cohesive and aesthetically pleasing.
- [ ] I have updated all elements (images, backgrounds, text, buttons, etc.) to use the new colors consistently.
- [ ] The new theme maintains readability (e.g., sufficient contrast) and visual balance.

### ✍️ Copywriting

This tests your ability to:

- Read and understand therapist profiles
- Translate that information into clear website copy

### The Task

- Rewrite **all homepage copy** (headlines, subtext, button text)
- Strictly base all copy on [Dr. Maya Reynolds’ Profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)
- This includes: H1 headings, subtext below headings, services (choose and describe three relevant services), about section, FAQs, and any other text elements.
- SEO optimized for the local region in the profile, following best SEO practices (e.g., include relevant keywords in H1, headings, and body text; use location-specific terms; ensure natural keyword integration for search engines).

You may use AI tools, but **final judgment and refinement must be yours**.

### Copy Must Be Based On:

- Therapist bio and approach
- Modalities and specialties
- Client focus and populations
- Location and practice type
- Any other information form their Psychology Today profile

**Checklist for Copywriting:**

- [ ] All copy is derived from the [profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)
- [ ] Headings (e.g., H1) include SEO keywords relevant to the main specialty and location mentioned in the [profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)
- [ ] Services section features three services pulled from the [profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing), with descriptions.
- [ ] About, FAQs, and other sections are tailored to the [profile's](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing) details.
- [ ] Copy is SEO-optimized: natural keyword usage, location mentions, and best practices applied.

### 🖼️ Images

- Replace all images with new ones

Images should:

- Match the new theme and color palette
- Feel intentional (not random stock photos)
- Support the message of each section
- Be relevant to the section and the theme

Image choice is a **major evaluation factor**.

**Checklist for Images:**

- [ ] All images have been replaced with new ones that match the new color palette and theme.
- [ ] Images are relevant to the services and content from the [profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing).
- [ ] Images feel intentional and support the section's message
- [ ] I have added Maya’s picture and created a bio using from her [profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)

### **Part 3: Add One New Custom Section (Creative Thinking Test)**

Create **one completely new section** that does NOT exist in the original template: an **“Our Office”** section.

This section should:

- Fit naturally into the homepage flow
- Use office details mentioned on the [profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)
- Use consistent spacing, typography, and styling from the rest of the site
- Feel warm, human, and trustworthy—aligned with a therapy practice
- Highlight the therapist’s physical practice space (even if the profile implies in-person or hybrid sessions)

**Section Requirements:**

- Section title (e.g., *Our Office*, *A Calm Space for Healing*, etc.)
- Short supporting copy describing the office environment and what clients can expect
- 2–3 images representing:
  - The therapy office / counseling space (Should be taken from their profile)

Optional supporting details such as:

- Location mention
- In-person / hybrid session availability
- Emphasis on safety, comfort, and privacy

**Checklist for New Section:**

- [ ] I have added an **“Our Office”** section that does not exist in the original template.
- [ ] The section includes a clear heading and thoughtfully written supporting copy.
- [ ] I have used relevant images of the office from the [Dr. Maya Reynolds Profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)
- [ ] The copy and office images aligns with [Dr. Maya Reynolds Profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)
- [ ] **[IMP]** The section integrates seamlessly with the site’s theme, design, spacing, and typography.

---

### **Part 4: Video Walkthrough (Communication Test)**

Record a 5** minute **[**loom**](http://loom.com/)** video** assuming you are giving a demo to the client.

- Treat this as demoing the first draft of their requested website.
- Cover desktop & mobile walkthroughs.
- Explain your design choices, what you've done with their information ([Dr. Maya Reynolds profile](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-IJVKEjuqV9CTd9QH16UNHJ7SQfdiweS4oAIZ8vmgHU/edit?usp=sharing)), and key features.
- Use non-technical language to explain what they are looking at and why it benefits them.

Good English, clarity, and confidence matter here.

**Checklist for Video:**

- [ ] Video is 5 minutes long.
- [ ] Presented as a client demo to an imaginary therapist Dr. Maya Reynolds: "Here's your new website draft," focusing on benefits and how it uses their profile info.
- [ ] Includes walkthrough of desktop and mobile views.
- [ ] Explains design choices and copy in **non-technical terms**.
- [ ] Clear, confident communication **without** technical **jargon.
